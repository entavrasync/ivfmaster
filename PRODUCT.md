# IVFMaster

## Register

brand

## Users

Patients, couples and families seeking understandable fertility information, clinic updates and opportunities to attend sessions. Clinicians also need to scan subjects, authorship, schedules and event formats quickly. Public pages serve readers in English and Marathi on phones and larger screens.

## Product Purpose

Help people understand the care team, find reliable information and decide their next step without pressure. News communicates clinic updates; Events makes dates, attendance and availability easy to scan.

## Brand Personality

Calm, reassuring and precise. Continue the established IVFMaster identity, as confirmed by the user for the News and Events redesign. The existing About design specification provides the broader voice and content guardrails.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic hospital marketing, invented outcomes, urgency tactics, cramped thumbnails, inconsistent content widths and loosely arranged content without a clear hierarchy.

News and Events use defined tiles with thin borders and compact shadows at about 10% opacity, plus warm surfaces and comfortable inset spacing. Avoid thick colored outlines, conspicuous drop shadows and floating tile effects.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the established identity and locale-aware navigation.
- Give each page a clear primary story or event, followed by an ordered collection.
- Use shared content constraints and predictable spacing to make reading comfortable.
- Present actual editorial content honestly, including missing images and event status.
- Make authorship and practical event details prominent without inventing clinical claims or credentials.
- Keep filtering, links and essential event information usable at every screen size.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use WCAG AA contrast as the implementation baseline. Support keyboard focus, semantic headings, readable line lengths, touch controls, reduced motion and longer Marathi labels.
